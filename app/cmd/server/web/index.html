Best price: the better line for the bettor wins first (higher spread, lower over, higher under), then better odds; ties go to the most recently updated book. Suspended prices and stale or down books are never best.

Polymarket is a prediction market. Its price is what buying that outcome costs right now (the best ask), converted to odds. It ignores fees and how much is available at that price, and Polymarket is not open to every region.

Cells flash green when the price got better for the bettor and red when it got worse; the struck-through price is the one it replaced. Ages are measured against server time.
